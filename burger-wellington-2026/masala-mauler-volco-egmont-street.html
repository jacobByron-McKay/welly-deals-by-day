Masala Mauler

Volco - Egmont Street · 11 Egmont Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $26

Masala Mauler is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Volco - Egmont Street at 11 Egmont Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $26. Crumbed chicken thigh, with date and tamarind chutney, Penray Gardens chilli and coriander salsa, and peanut and coconut crumb in a Volco milk bun. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $36 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
