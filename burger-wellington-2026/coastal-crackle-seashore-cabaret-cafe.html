Coastal Crackle

Seashore Cabaret Cafe · 160 The Esplanade, Petone, Wellington · $29

Coastal Crackle is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Seashore Cabaret Cafe at 160 The Esplanade, Petone, Wellington, priced at $29. Pork belly, with mango salsa, coleslaw, pork crackling, pineapple, Apostle Hot Sauce, and sriracha honey mayonnaise in a brioche bun, with steak fries. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $39 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
