Marites

Marites is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Lazy Juan at G/F 155 The Terrace Wellington Central (RNZ Building), Wellington CBD, Wellington, priced at $25. Smashed beef tapa patty, with Zany Zeus ube cream cheese, baby rocket, and sinamak balsamic reduction, and calamansi honey aioli in an Arobake brioche bun, with garlic fried rice noodles and buttered corn. It is matched with Chillax (XPA), $35 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
