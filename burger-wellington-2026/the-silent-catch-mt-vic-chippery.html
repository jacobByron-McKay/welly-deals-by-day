The Silent Catch

Mt Vic Chippery · 5 Majoribanks Street, Mount Victoria, Wellington · $24

The Silent Catch is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Mt Vic Chippery at 5 Majoribanks Street, Mount Victoria, Wellington, priced at $24. Beer-battered warehou fillet, with red cabbage, onion, house-pickled butternut, curry leaf aioli, and tamarind glaze in an Arobake brioche bun, with curry leaf salted Agria fries and warm chickpea dip. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
