3 Little Pigs Story

Wildfire Churrascaria · 60 Tory Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $22

3 Little Pigs Story is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Wildfire Churrascaria at 60 Tory Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $22. Wildfire sausage, bacon, and rotisserie pork scotch in a cranberry and red wine glaze, with provolone, apple coleslaw, and chipotle mayonnaise in an Arobake sage ciabatta, with truffle polenta fries. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $29 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
