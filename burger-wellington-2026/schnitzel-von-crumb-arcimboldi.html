Schnitzel Von Crumb

Arcimboldi · 24 Dundas Street, Seatoun, Wellington · $28

Schnitzel Von Crumb is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Arcimboldi at 24 Dundas Street, Seatoun, Wellington, priced at $28. Crumbed beef patty, with housemade sauerkraut, dill pickle mayonnaise, Polish beetroot ćwikła, and gruyère crisp in an Arobake poppy seed brioche bun, with German potato salad. It is matched with Red Rascal (Red IPA), $40 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
