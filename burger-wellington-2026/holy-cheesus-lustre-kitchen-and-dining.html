Holy Cheesus

Lustre Kitchen and Dining · 7-13 Seaview Road, Paraparaumu Beach, Wellington · $25

Holy Cheesus is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Lustre Kitchen and Dining at 7-13 Seaview Road, Paraparaumu Beach, Wellington, priced at $25. Conscious Valley beef brisket, with Ōtaki cucumber pickles, Double Vision Brewery Hazy beer cheese, and kimchi mayo in a housemade sourdough brioche bagel bun. It is matched with Mouth Party (Hazy Pale), $35 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
