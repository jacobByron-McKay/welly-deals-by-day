Heard It Thru The Grain Brine

Fork & Brewer · 20a Bond Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $28

Heard It Thru The Grain Brine is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Fork & Brewer at 20a Bond Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $28. Fork & Brewer koji rice ale-brined chicken thigh, with soy and sesame coleslaw, pickled cucumber and carrot, pickled red onion, crushed peanuts, charred corn, coriander-lime salsa, smoked pineapple, and mandarin aioli in a milk bap, with shoestring fries and ketchup. It is matched with Chillax (XPA), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
