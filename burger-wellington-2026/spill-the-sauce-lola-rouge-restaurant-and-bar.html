Spill The Sauce

Lola Rouge Restaurant & Bar · 213-223 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $24

Spill The Sauce is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Lola Rouge Restaurant & Bar at 213-223 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $24. Sichuan chilli chicken thigh, with pickled carrot and daikon, shredded iceberg, and kewpie mayonnaise in a Dough Bakery sesame milk bun, with nori-dusted waffle fries. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $35 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
