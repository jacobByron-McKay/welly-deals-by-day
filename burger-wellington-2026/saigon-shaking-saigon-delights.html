Saigon Shaking

Saigon Delights · 81 Victoria Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $28

Saigon Shaking is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Saigon Delights at 81 Victoria Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $28. Garlic and soy seasoned sirloin patty with mixed greens, aged cheddar, gherkins, tomato, red onion and sriracha mayo on a Shelly Bay Baker brioche bun, with fries.  It is matched with Naughty Haze (Hazy IPA), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
