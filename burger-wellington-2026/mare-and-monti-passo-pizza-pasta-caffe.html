Mare & Monti is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Passo Pizza Pasta Caffe' at 159 Kapiti Road, Paraparaumu, Wellington, priced at $32. Prawn and calamari patty, with pork belly, roasted pepper, pickled red onion, provolone, stracciatella, rocket, nduja butter, and paprika mayonnaise in a housemade pizza dough bun, with a potato choux puff. It is matched with Chillax (XPA), $44 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
