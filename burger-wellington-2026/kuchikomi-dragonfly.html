Kuchikomi

Dragonfly · 70 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington · $28

Kuchikomi is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Dragonfly at 70 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $28. Beef and pork hambāgu patty, with house-pickled daikon, shaved savoy cabbage, American cheddar, prune tonkatsu, and yuzu kewpie in an Asian Food Republic wasabi crumb brioche bun, with coconut salt crisps. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $39 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
