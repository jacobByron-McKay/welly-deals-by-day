The Wagyu Affair

Boundary Tap & Kitchen · 3 Raumati Road, Paraparaumu Beach, Wellington · $29

The Wagyu Affair is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Boundary Tap & Kitchen at 3 Raumati Road, Paraparaumu Beach, Wellington, priced at $29. Wagyu beef, with battered onion rings, Prana Greens microherbs, coleslaw, truffle mushroom sauce, and roasted red pepper mayonnaise in a brioche bun, with shoestring fries and aioli. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $40 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
