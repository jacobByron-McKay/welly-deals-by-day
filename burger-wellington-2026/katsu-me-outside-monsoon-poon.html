Katsu Me Outside

Monsoon Poon · 12 Blair Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $22

Katsu Me Outside is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Monsoon Poon at 12 Blair Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $22. Woody's Free Range Farm pork and prawn patty, with mirin-pickled cucumber, shredded cabbage, lettuce, and katsu-spiced mayo in a Brezelmania milk bun, with togarashi-seasoned crisps. It is matched with Mouth Party (Hazy Pale), $35 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
