Bovine Meets Coastline

Pravda Café and Grill · 107 Customhouse Quay, Te Aro, Wellington · $34

Bovine Meets Coastline is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Pravda Café and Grill at 107 Customhouse Quay, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $34. Beef patty and prawn cutlets, with gruyère, cos lettuce, pickles, thermidor sauce, and Dijon aioli in an Arobake potato bap bun, with steak-cut fries and chipotle mayonnaise. It is matched with Chillax (XPA), $49 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
