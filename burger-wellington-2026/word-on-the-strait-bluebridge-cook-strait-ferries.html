Bluebridge Cook Strait Ferries · 50 Waterloo Quay, Pipitea, Wellington · $24

Word on the Strait is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Bluebridge Cook Strait Ferries at 50 Waterloo Quay, Pipitea, Wellington, priced at $24. Buttermilk and sriracha crumbed chicken breast, with Asian coleslaw, iceberg lettuce, Sichuan chilli and sesame crumb, BBQ sauce, and garlic mayonnaise in a Cottage Lane bun, with fries. It is matched with Mouth Party (Hazy Pale), $34 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
