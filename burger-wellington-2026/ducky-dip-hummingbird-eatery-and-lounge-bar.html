Ducky Dip

Hummingbird Eatery and Lounge Bar · 22 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington · $30

Ducky Dip is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Hummingbird Eatery and Lounge Bar at 22 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $30. Confit duck patty, with pickled radish, bitter leaves, orange reduction, and truffle mayonnaise in a housemade bun, with duck fat fries and duck au jus. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
