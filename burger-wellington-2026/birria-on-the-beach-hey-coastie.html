Birria On The Beach

Hey Coastie · 42 Tutere Street, Waikanae Beach, Wellington · $34

Birria On The Beach is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Hey Coastie at 42 Tutere Street, Waikanae Beach, Wellington, priced at $34. Beef patty and pulled birria beef, with corn tortilla, onion and coriander salsa, Apostle Tropical Charred Sweetcorn Salsa and spicy crema in a housemade brioche bun, with jalapeño poppers and birria consomé. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $44 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
