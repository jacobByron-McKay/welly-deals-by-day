Seekh No Further

Masala Cafe & Bar · 2-12 Allen Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $24

Seekh No Further is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Masala Cafe & Bar at 2-12 Allen Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $24. Tandoor-cooked lamb seekh kebab, with mint chutney, pickled red onion, cucumber, chaat masala, tamarind sauce, and papadum in a Pōneke Bakery sesame seed milk bun. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $37 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
