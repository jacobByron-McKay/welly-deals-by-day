Gossip Town

D4 on Featherston · 145 Featherston Street, Wellington CBD, Wellington · $28

Gossip Town is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from D4 on Featherston at 145 Featherston Street, Wellington CBD, Wellington, priced at $28. Beef and kransky patty, with sweet and spicy pickles, tasty cheese, lettuce, and bourbon bacon jam in an Arobake cheese and potato bun topped with a cheese-stuffed pepper, and seasoned fries with chipotle flame mayonnaise. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $36 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
