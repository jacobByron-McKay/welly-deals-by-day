The HogFather

Sweet Mother's Kitchen · 5 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington · $36

The HogFather is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Sweet Mother's Kitchen at 5 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $36. Pulled pork and bacon, with Swiss cheese, kewpie mayonnaise coleslaw, and smoky BBQ glaze in a housemade jalapeño corn waffle bun, with hot honey and straight-cut Cajun fries. It is matched with Expatriate (APA), $46 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
