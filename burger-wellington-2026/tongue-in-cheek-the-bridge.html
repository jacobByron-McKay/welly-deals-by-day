Tongue in Cheek

The Bridge · 28 Cambridge Terrace, Te Aro, Wellington · $29

Tongue in Cheek is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from The Bridge at 28 Cambridge Terrace, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $29. Beef cheek patty, with schnitzel tongue, Pickle & Pie bread and butter pickles, gruyère, sauerkraut, rocket, and wholegrain mustard aioli in a Brezelmania sesame seed brioche bun, with fries. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
