New Dynasty

Horseshoe Taproom · 4 Queen Street, Masterton, Wellington · $32

New Dynasty is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Horseshoe Taproom at 4 Queen Street, Masterton, Wellington, priced at $32. Roasted five-spice pork belly, pork and garlic chive patty, with Rebel Gardens charred cabbage coleslaw, smacked cucumber salad, pork crackling, LOUDER chilli oil, and hoisin mayonnaise in a housemade pan-fried bao bun, with Sichuan salted fries and pork bone broth. It is matched with Chillax (XPA), $42 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
