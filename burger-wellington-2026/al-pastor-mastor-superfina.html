Al Pastor Mastor

Superfina · 232 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $27

Al Pastor Mastor is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Superfina at 232 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $27. Al pastor pork, with Kāpiti smoked pepper jack cheese, agave-glazed jalapeño, fire-roasted pineapple, pickled red onion, and smoked chilli crema in a Cottage Lane potato bun with flaming mezcal, with fries and hot smoked queso and pico de gallo salsa. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $40 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
