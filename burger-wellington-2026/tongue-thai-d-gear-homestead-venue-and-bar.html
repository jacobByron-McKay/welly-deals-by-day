Tongue Thai’d

Gear Homestead Venue and Bar · 1 Okowai Road, Aotea, Wellington · $34

Tongue Thai’d is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Gear Homestead Venue and Bar at 1 Okowai Road, Aotea, Wellington, priced at $34. Beef patty, with Kāpiti smoked havarti, tamarind and makrut lime onion jam, laab-style herb coleslaw, pickled daikon and carrot, and Thai chilli paste mayonnaise in an Arobake brioche bun, with toasted rice, lime, and chilli salt fries and Thai chilli cheese sauce. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $45 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
