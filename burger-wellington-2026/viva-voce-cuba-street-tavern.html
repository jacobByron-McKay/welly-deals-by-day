Viva Voce

Cuba Street Tavern · 172 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $32

Viva Voce is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Cuba Street Tavern at 172 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $32. Crispy pork cheek, with beef tongue bacon, gem lettuce, Thai basil, smoked cheese, kawakawa BBQ sauce, and sriracha in a housemade brioche bun, with Sichuan seasoned shoestring fries. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $43 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
