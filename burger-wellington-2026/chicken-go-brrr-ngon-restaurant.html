Chicken Go Brrr

Ngon Restaurant · 2 Roxburgh Street, Mount Victoria, Wellington · $28

Chicken Go Brrr is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Ngon Restaurant at 2 Roxburgh Street, Mount Victoria, Wellington, priced at $28. Fried chicken, with pickled daikon, tomato, lettuce, jalapeño, fried garlic, scallion oil, garlic chilli fish sauce, and Vietnamese garlic mayonnaise in a Dough Bakery milk bun, with Agria fries and chilli mayonnaise. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $39 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
