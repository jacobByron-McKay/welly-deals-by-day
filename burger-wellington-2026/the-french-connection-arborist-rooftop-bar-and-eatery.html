The French Connection

Arborist Rooftop Bar & Eatery · 166 Willis Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $34

The French Connection is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Arborist Rooftop Bar & Eatery at 166 Willis Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $34. Beef patty and striploin steak, with Woodhaven Farms parsley, garlic butter, mustard aioli, and peppercorn sauce in a brioche bun, with shoestring fries and béarnaise. It is matched with The Classic (Lager), $44 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
