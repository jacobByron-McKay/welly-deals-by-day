Jakarta Meets Tokyo

Peloton Bar + Eatery · Inside the terminal at Wellington Airport, 28 Stewart Duff Drive, Rongotai, Wellington · $23

Jakarta Meets Tokyo is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Peloton Bar + Eatery at Inside the terminal at Wellington Airport, 28 Stewart Duff Drive, Rongotai, Wellington, priced at $23. Smashed chicken thigh, with green chilli sambal, torched mozzarella, lavender hot honey, and kewpie mayonnaise in a Shelly Bay Baker milk bun, with fries and curry sauce. It is matched with Mouth Party (Hazy Pale), $30 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
