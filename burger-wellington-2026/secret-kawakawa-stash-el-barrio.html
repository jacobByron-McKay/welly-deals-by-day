Secret Kawakawa Stash

El Barrio · 35 Dixon Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $32

Secret Kawakawa Stash is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from El Barrio at 35 Dixon Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $32. Kawakawa fried chicken thigh and a fermented mānuka honey and habanero glaze, with Zany Zeus halloumi, fennel and apple coleslaw, and Fix & Fogg smoke and fire aioli in an Arobake brioche bun, with fries. It is matched with Red Rascal (Red IPA), $40 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
