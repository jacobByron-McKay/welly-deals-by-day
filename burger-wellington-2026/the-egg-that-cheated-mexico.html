The Egg That Cheated

Mexico · 41 Dixon Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $28

The Egg That Cheated is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Mexico at 41 Dixon Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $28. Panko-crumbed avocado, plant-based bacon, hibiscus and lime dressed lettuce, el jitomate sauce, spicy mango salsa, jalapeño, chipotle mayonnaise, and Apostle Hot Sauce chilli oil in a brioche bun. It is matched with Naughty Haze (Hazy IPA), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
