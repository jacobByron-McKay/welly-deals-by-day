Smoke 'n' Mirrors

Otto Champagneria and Beer Hall · 125 Featherston Street, Wellington CBD, Wellington · $24

Smoke 'n' Mirrors is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Otto Champagneria and Beer Hall at 125 Featherston Street, Wellington CBD, Wellington, priced at $24. Mushroom House oyster mushroom smash patty, with Kāpiti smoked pimento cheese, pickled green tomato, maple facon, tobacco onion, bourbon BBQ glaze, rocket, and moonshine mayonnaise in a Cottage Lane potato bun, with speakeasy spud skins and garlic salt. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $37 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
