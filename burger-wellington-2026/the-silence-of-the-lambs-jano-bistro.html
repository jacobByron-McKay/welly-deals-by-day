The Silence of the Lambs

Jano Bistro · 270 Willis Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $32

The Silence of the Lambs is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Jano Bistro at 270 Willis Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $32. Conscious Valley lamb patty, with baby spinach, pickled red onion, and Whitestone brie slice, and fermented apricot ketchup and chilli crisp in a Good Grief stirato bun, with dauphine potato and lamb gravy. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $46 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
