Ayam What I Am

Chapman Taylor's Cafe & Inn · 113 Molesworth Street, Thorndon, Wellington · $30

Ayam What I Am is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Chapman Taylor's Cafe & Inn at 113 Molesworth Street, Thorndon, Wellington, priced at $30. Turks Chicken thigh in Rica Rica sauce, with lettuce, tomato, pickled cucumber, coriander, chicken skin, and sriracha aioli in a toasted brioche bun, with twice-cooked fries and sambal. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $42 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
