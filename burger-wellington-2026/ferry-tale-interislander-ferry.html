Ferry Tale

Interislander Ferry · 1 Aotea Quay, Pipitea, Wellington · $15

Ferry Tale is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Interislander Ferry at 1 Aotea Quay, Pipitea, Wellington, priced at $15. Wairarapa lamb, with smoked scamorza, Zany Zeus roasted garlic yoghurt, pickled red onion, rocket, and spiced tomato relish in a Pōneke Bakery pretzel bun. (Also available at their pop up outside the Wellington Railway Station on Thursdays & Fridays 11am-2pm) It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $27 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
