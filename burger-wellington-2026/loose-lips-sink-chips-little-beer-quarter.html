Loose Lips Sink Chips

Little Beer Quarter · 6 Edward Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $25

Loose Lips Sink Chips is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Little Beer Quarter at 6 Edward Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $25. Pulled pork shoulder, with Kāpiti smoked cheddar, jerk coleslaw, pickles, onion crisps, potato crisps, and rum BBQ sauce in a seeded brioche bun, with paprika-salted crinkle-cut chips and jerk mayonnaise. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $37 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
