0800 THE WORD is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Zephyr Cafe at Tākina, 217 Wakefield Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $19. Rocket Fuel glazed smashed beef patty, with pickled cabbage and beetroot coleslaw, smoked cheese, and Apostle Hot Honey spiked tomato sauce in a sesame seed bun, with cheese onion salted fries and kiwi onion dip. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $27 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
