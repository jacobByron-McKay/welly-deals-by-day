Veni, Vidi, Vici

Nolita · 203 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $25

Veni, Vidi, Vici is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Nolita at 203 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $25. Plan*t bolognese smash patty, with housemade vegan mozzarella, crumbed eggplant parmigiana, tomato chilli jam, pickled zucchini, and béchamel in a Cottage Lane garlic buttered potato bun, with parmesan salted spiral pasta fries and pesto mayonnaise. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
