Kerabu and Tell!

Macha, Jom Tapau! · Unit 206, Left Bank, Te Aro, Wellington · $27

Kerabu and Tell! is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Macha, Jom Tapau! at Unit 206, Left Bank, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $27. Torch ginger flower chicken patty, with green apple kerabu salad, coconut fish kerisik, pickled ginger, and salted egg percik sauce in a Le Ciel Bakery smoke-glazed blue pea bun, with garlic crackers and sambal tumis. It is matched with Tinyken (Non-Alc Hazy IPA), $35 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
