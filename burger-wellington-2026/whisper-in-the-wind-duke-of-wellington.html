Whisper in the Wind

Duke Of Wellington · 29 Waterloo Quay, Pipitea, Wellington CBD, Wellington · $29

Whisper in the Wind is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Duke Of Wellington at 29 Waterloo Quay, Pipitea, Wellington CBD, Wellington, priced at $29. Venison and bacon patty, with pickled blueberry compote, Kāpiti havarti, pork crackling, and Marmite mayo in an Arobake potato bun topped with beetroot sprinkle and The Bond Store kawakawa gin spritz, with porcini salt Agria tornado fries and truffle mayo. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $42 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
