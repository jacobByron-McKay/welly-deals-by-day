Le Ganh

Pho Sho Porirua · 20 Parumoana Street, Porirua Central, Wellington · $24

Le Ganh is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Pho Sho Porirua at 20 Parumoana Street, Porirua Central, Wellington, priced at $24. Smashed beef patty, with pickled carrot and daikon, pickled onion, coriander, fried shallots, pho noodles, pho beef glaze, and pho mayonnaise in a Brezelmania sesame brioche bun, with pho-spiced shoestring fries. It is matched with Tinyken (Non-Alc Hazy IPA), $31 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
