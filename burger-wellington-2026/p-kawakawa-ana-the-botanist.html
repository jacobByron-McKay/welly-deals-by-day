Pō Kawakawa Ana

The Botanist · 219 Onepu Road, Lyall Bay, Wellington · $23

Pō Kawakawa Ana is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from The Botanist at 219 Onepu Road, Lyall Bay, Wellington, priced at $23. L&P-battered kūmara fritter and Mushroom House oyster mushroom "scallops", with foraged kawakawa and Granny Smith coleslaw, iceberg lettuce, kiwi and green apple chutney, and Kiwi onion dip in a Cottage Lane potato bun, with fairy bread-salted chips and marmite gravy. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
