The Sicilian Cowboy

Ombra · 199 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington · $27

The Sicilian Cowboy is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Ombra at 199 Cuba Street, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $27. Beef patty and porchetta, with caramelised pineapple, scamorza, pickled red onion, and Karma Cola BBQ sauce in a Wellington Sourdough sesame bun. It is matched with Loud & Clear (DDH Bright IPA), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
