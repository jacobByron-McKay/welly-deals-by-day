Bao Wow

Hideout · 11c Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington · $19

Bao Wow is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Hideout at 11c Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $19. Slow-cooked pork belly, with bacon, lettuce, wonton cracker, 26 Seasons microgreens, sriracha mayo, and Peking sauce in a Dough Bakery brioche bun, with Agria fries. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $28 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
