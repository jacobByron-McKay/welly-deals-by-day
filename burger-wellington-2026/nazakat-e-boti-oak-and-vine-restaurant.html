Nazakat‑e‑Boti

Oak & Vine Restaurant · 89 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington · $26

Nazakat‑e‑Boti is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Oak & Vine Restaurant at 89 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $26. Nawabi masala beef boti, with housemade Kaladi cheese, onion, tomato and capsicum chaat, and mint and coriander chutney in a Brezelmania brioche bun, with shoestring fries. It is matched with The Classic (Lager), $36 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
