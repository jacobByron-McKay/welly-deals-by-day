Spilling the Beans

Get Fixed Bicycle Cafe & Panhead Bar · 20 Parumoana Street, Porirua Central, Wellington · $30

Spilling the Beans is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Get Fixed Bicycle Cafe & Panhead Bar at 20 Parumoana Street, Porirua Central, Wellington, priced at $30. 12-hour slow-cooked brisket, with apple and fennel coleslaw, deep-fried pickle spears, Ripe Beans coffee-infused BBQ sauce, and aioli in a Ripe Coffee dusted Nada Bakery brioche bun, with potato and kūmara chips. It is matched with Smooth Operator (Cream Ale), $42 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
