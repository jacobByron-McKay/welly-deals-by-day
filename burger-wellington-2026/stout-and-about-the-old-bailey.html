Stout & About

The Old Bailey · 101 Lambton Quay, Wellington CBD, Wellington · $32

Stout & About is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from The Old Bailey at 101 Lambton Quay, Wellington CBD, Wellington, priced at $32. Stout-braised beef cheek, with Kāpiti aged cheddar, caramelised onion jam, pickles, lettuce, and horseradish cream in a Pōneke Bakery brioche bun, with stout gravy dip and fries. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $38 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
