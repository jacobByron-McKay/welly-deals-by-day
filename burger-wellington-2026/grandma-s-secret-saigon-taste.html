Grandma's Secret

Saigon Taste · 17 Majoribanks Street, Mount Victoria, Wellington · $27

Grandma's Secret is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Saigon Taste at 17 Majoribanks Street, Mount Victoria, Wellington, priced at $27. Fish and prawn patty, with pickled carrot and daikon, lettuce, cucumber, Vietnamese mint, and housemade lemongrass sauce in a Dough Bakery sesame milk bun, with prawn crackers and a prawn spring roll. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $37 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
