Lamb-da Rassa Pao

Bellbird Eatery · The Dowse Art Museum, 45 Laing's Rd, Hutt Central, Wellington · $30

Lamb-da Rassa Pao is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Bellbird Eatery at The Dowse Art Museum, 45 Laing's Rd, Hutt Central, Wellington, priced at $30. Lamb smash patty and pulled lamb shoulder, with cheddar, pickled onion, Zany Zeus mint yoghurt chakka, lettuce, LOUDER peanut chilli thecha, and thecha mayonnaise in a Clareville Bakery sorghum milk pav bun, with spice fries. It is matched with Schandalous (Shandy), $43 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
