Second Mention

Intercontinental Hotel Wellington · 2 Grey Street, Wellington CBD, Wellington · $29

Second Mention is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Intercontinental Hotel Wellington at 2 Grey Street, Wellington CBD, Wellington, priced at $29. Wagyu menchi katsu, with soy-mirin cured egg yolk, wong bok coleslaw, kewpie curry mayonnaise, and caviar in a Brezelmania bun. It is matched with Tinyken (Non-Alc Hazy IPA), $41 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
