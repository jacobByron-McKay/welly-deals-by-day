A Little Less Conversation

The Tasting Room · 2 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington · $30

A Little Less Conversation is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from The Tasting Room at 2 Courtenay Place, Te Aro, Wellington, priced at $30. Two smashed beef patties, with American cheese, bacon, Montfoort stroopwafel, Fix & Fogg peanut butter, and spicy chilli jam in an Arobake seeded milk bun, with shoestring fries. It is matched with Chin-Wag (Fruited Hazy), $42 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
