Kiss my Rump Cap!

Nara · 14 Memorial Square, Martinborough, Wellington · $32

Kiss my Rump Cap! is a Burger Wellington 2026 entry from Nara at 14 Memorial Square, Martinborough, Wellington, priced at $32. Picanha beef patty, with black bean chimichurri, Rebel Gardens cavolo nero, orange, cassava crisps, and aji verde in a housemade brioche bun, with twice-cooked steak-cut fries. It is matched with Naughty Haze (Hazy IPA), $44 as a combo. It is available from 3–23 August 2026 during Visa Wellington On a Plate.
