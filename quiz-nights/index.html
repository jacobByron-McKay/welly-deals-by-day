How many quiz nights are there in Wellington?

46 that we can verify, across 42 venues. Each one on this page shows the night of the week, the time and the venue.

How are these deals verified?

Every deal is checked by hand against the venue's own website, Facebook page or Instagram before it is published, and each record stores the source and the date it was last confirmed. Anything that cannot be confirmed with a day, a time and a price is not published at all. Nothing is scraped into the site unchecked.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free, and no. There is no app, no sign-up and no account. There is also no paid placement, so no venue can buy its way up the page.
